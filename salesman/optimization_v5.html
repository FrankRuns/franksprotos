Why each store gets its cadence — marginal $ per visit vs. drive cost

Each bar = the revenue the next visit adds. Green = clears its drive cost. Orange = positive but below cost. Red = negative (destroys value). Dashed line = per-visit drive cost. A store is visited as many times as its bars stay green.

Storrs vs Litchfield: both ~45–53 mi out, but Storrs' 4th visit still earns $71 > its $44 drive (shallow curve, k=0.6) → 4×. Litchfield's 2nd visit is already −$47 (steep, k=1.9) → 1×. Distance doesn't set cadence — how fast marginal revenue decays does.
