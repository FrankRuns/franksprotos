U.S. preserves and spreads, illustrative scenario. Annual volume is inferred from a publicly reported historical retail sales benchmark, assumed average retail price, and a growth multiplier. This is not a current sales forecast or an Andros disclosure.

Hypothesis: preserves are a year-round pantry staple with moderate holiday gifting and late-summer canning/baking bumps. The pattern is conjectural, not measured seasonality. Andros reported Bonne Maman U.S. retail sales above $115.6M in 2020; the scenario uses $115.6M as a conservative starting benchmark, not current revenue. Bonne Maman branded preserves are imported, so do not assume these jars are made in Mount Jackson.
