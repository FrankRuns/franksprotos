How the model works: it tries every combination of allowed sites (up to the maximum), then assigns each store's demand to the cheapest DC with spare capacity using an exact min-cost flow. A new DC costs $30M upfront (annualized at $3.06M a year over 20 years at 8%) plus $4M a year to operate, with 400k units of capacity. Chicago holds 2,300k units. Freight is straight-line miles × units × rate. Illustrative synthetic data.
