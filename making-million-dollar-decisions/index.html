03 · AI as an analytical partner

Ask better questions. Find blind spots.

NETWORK ANALYST Illustrative conversation · synthetic scenario
How many warehouses do I have, and how utilized are they?
AI You have 2 DCs: Chicago (88% utilized) and Atlanta (94%). Atlanta has little headroom for a demand spike.
Which warehouse should I open? What happens to cost and service?
AI In this illustrative run, Denver reduces transportation cost and average distance to stores. But would those savings cover a $30M investment plus annual operating costs? Ask the solver for the full business case.
What blind spots do I have in this analysis?
AI The optimization assumes known demand and travel costs. Use simulation to stress-test demand spikes, disruptions and lead-time variability before committing.

Mock dialogue, not a live AI model or validated solver output. Activity → Interrogate the solution and propose a stress test.

The story matters. The opening wins attention.

The Hero’s Journey gives you the full arc. A short executive pitch rarely has that luxury.

THE SHORT FORM

Micro-stories

Same destination, faster entry. Choose the opening that earns attention:

INSIGHT“We thought transportation was the problem. It was actually capacity.”
PROOF“Would you invest $30M if it saved $10M every year?”
PROBLEM“When demand spikes, stores in the West run out first.”
